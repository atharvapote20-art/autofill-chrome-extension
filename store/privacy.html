Privacy Policy

Secure Form Vault · Last updated: August 6, 2026

Secure Form Vault (“the Extension”) is a Chrome extension that stores form-fill profiles and snippets in an encrypted vault on your device. This policy explains what data is handled and what is not.

Data we store on your device

  • Encrypted vault: profiles, field values, and snippets sealed with AES-GCM using a key derived from your master password (PBKDF2). Ciphertext is stored in Chrome extension storage on your browser profile.
  • Settings: preferences such as session timeout, fill behavior, and optional third-party API keys you enter yourself (Gemini / Groq). API keys are stored in local extension storage and are not placed inside the encrypted vault.
  • Session state: while unlocked, the Extension may keep session material in chrome.storage.session so the vault can stay unlocked across service-worker restarts until you lock it or quit the browser.
  • Last-used profile id: a non-sensitive identifier used to choose which profile to fill with.

What we do not collect

  • We do not operate a Secure Form Vault backend that receives your vault contents.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Your master password is not uploaded to any server by the Extension.
  • Analytics / advertising SDKs are not bundled with the Extension for the purpose of tracking you across sites.

How filling works on websites

To fill forms, the Extension runs a content script on http(s) pages you visit (as permitted by Chrome). It reads field labels and related page text needed for matching, and writes values from your unlocked vault into form controls when you request a fill. This processing happens in your browser.

Optional smart fill (Gemini / Groq)

If you enable Smart fill and provide your own API key, the Extension may send field labels / matching text only (not your master password, and not a dump of your entire vault) to Google Gemini or Groq so those services can suggest which profile keys map to which fields. Values are still applied locally. Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Smart fill is off by default.

Permissions (why they exist)

  • storage — save encrypted vault and settings on device.
  • activeTab / scripting — inject or talk to the page helper when filling.
  • alarms — enforce session timeout / auto-lock.
  • Host access to http(s) pages — detect and fill forms on websites you use.
  • generativelanguage.googleapis.com / api.groq.com — only used if you enable the corresponding smart-fill provider and supply a key.

Backups

If you export a vault backup, you receive an encrypted JSON file. Anyone with that file still needs the correct passphrase to decrypt it. Keep exports private.

Children’s privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the Extension’s behavior changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change accordingly.
